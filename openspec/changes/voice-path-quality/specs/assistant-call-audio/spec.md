# Spec Delta

## Purpose

Makes the assistant hear everything the session gate passes it, in order and without holes,
and makes its answer play steadily in the channel, so recognition and speech quality do not
depend on clock drift or network jitter.

## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every gated frame reaches the call in order
Each frame the session gate passes toward the assistant SHALL be sent into the call exactly
once, in the order it was passed, paced at one frame per 20 ms. A frame SHALL be dropped only
when more than 100 ms of audio is waiting, and every such drop SHALL be counted.

#### Scenario: Gate ticks drift against the send clock
- **WHEN** the gate passes 500 frames with tick intervals varying between 5 ms and 35 ms
- **THEN** the call receives all 500 frames in the same order and no frame is counted as dropped

#### Scenario: Backlog beyond 100 ms
- **WHEN** more than five frames are waiting to be sent
- **THEN** the oldest frames are dropped down to five, and the drop counter increases by the number dropped

### Requirement: The stream toward the call never stalls
While a call is connected, one packet SHALL be sent every 20 ms. When no gated frame is
waiting, the packet SHALL carry silence and the event SHALL be counted as an input underrun.

#### Scenario: Gate sends nothing for a moment
- **WHEN** no frame arrives from the gate for 100 ms during a call
- **THEN** five packets of silence are sent, the input underrun counter increases by five, and the packet timestamps stay continuous

### Requirement: The answer plays in order without gaps
Packets of the assistant's voice SHALL be played in sequence order one 20 ms frame per tick,
after a short buffer that absorbs network jitter. A packet arriving after its turn has been
played SHALL be discarded and counted as late.

#### Scenario: Packets arrive out of order
- **WHEN** packets 10, 12, 11, 13 arrive in that order within the buffer time
- **THEN** they are played as 10, 11, 12, 13

#### Scenario: Packet arrives too late
- **WHEN** packet 20 arrives after frame 20 has already been played
- **THEN** it is discarded, the late counter increases, and playback continues with 21

### Requirement: Lost packets are concealed
When a packet is missing at its turn, the frame SHALL be reconstructed from the next
packet's in-band redundancy when that packet is available, and otherwise generated by the
codec's loss concealment; each concealed frame SHALL be counted.

#### Scenario: One packet lost
- **WHEN** packet 31 never arrives and packet 32 is already buffered
- **THEN** frame 31 is decoded from packet 32's redundancy, the concealed counter increases by one, and no silence gap is heard

#### Scenario: Several packets lost
- **WHEN** packets 40 to 42 never arrive
- **THEN** three concealed frames are played and the concealed counter increases by three

### Requirement: Call audio counters
Each call SHALL count frames sent, input drops, input underruns, packets received, late
packets, concealed frames, playout underruns and output queue drops. The counters SHALL be
in the status while the call runs and SHALL be written as one log line when the call ends.

#### Scenario: End of a call
- **WHEN** a call ends
- **THEN** one log line names the guild and every counter of that call with its value

#### Scenario: Clean local network
- **WHEN** a ten-second question is sent through a call on a loopback connection
- **THEN** the input drop, late and concealed counters are zero
